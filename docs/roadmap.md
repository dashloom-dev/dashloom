# Dashloom Community roadmap

- Keep self-hosting reliable and documented.
- Expand privacy-minimized connectors and community-reviewed Agent Skills.
- Improve deterministic evidence quality, product scoping, and citations.
- Strengthen backup, portable export, migration, and restore verification.
- Keep BYOK execution, local schedules, and the five dashboard presets independent of hosted services.

Hosted product planning is intentionally maintained outside this public repository.
