# Dashloom Community product strategy

Dashloom Community is the public self-hosted core for operators who want product intelligence while retaining control of infrastructure, data, schedules, and model-provider agreements.

The loop is: connect data, normalize evidence, calculate deterministic metrics, detect material movement, run a BYOK specialist, preserve citations, and turn findings into reports or actions.

All five dashboard presets and the extension SDKs remain public. Commercial hosted infrastructure is developed separately in a private repository and must not be imported into, referenced by, or required by this codebase.
