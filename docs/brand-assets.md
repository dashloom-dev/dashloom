# Dashloom brand assets

## Idea

The mark shows five incoming product signals woven through a central crossing into three outgoing paths. The weave expresses Dashloom's role: connecting evidence, explaining change, and guiding action.

## Core colors

- Ink: `#ECF8F4`
- Tile: `#26292F`
- Tile edge: `#353940`
- Mint: `#20D4AE`
- Cyan: `#17C7C0`
- Signal blue: `#18B9F1`

## Asset map

- `public/brand/logo-mark.png`: canonical primary symbol, cropped directly from the approved artwork
- `public/brand/logo-lockup.svg`: light wordmark for dark backgrounds
- `public/brand/logo-lockup-dark.svg`: dark wordmark for light backgrounds
- `public/favicon-16x16.png` and `public/favicon-32x32.png`: browser fallbacks
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: iOS home-screen icon
- `public/android-chrome-192x192.png` and `public/android-chrome-512x512.png`: installable web-app icons
- `public/og-dashloom.png`: English social preview
- `public/og-zh.png`: Chinese social preview

## Usage

Keep clear space around the mark equal to one quarter of the tile width. Use the full lockup above 140 px wide and the standalone mark below that size. Do not remove the tile, simplify the central weave, change the path geometry or colors, add extra glow, or rotate the mark.

Run `npm run brand:generate` after changing the canonical PNG, social-card background, or social-card copy.
